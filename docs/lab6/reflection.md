# Lab 6 Reflection

Separating the responder behind a `Protocol` made swapping StubLLM for Template trivial and confirmed that routing, fee, and office logic are fully independent of the generation layer. The eval harness revealed that both paths satisfy all four cases, which gives confidence that a real LLM can be dropped in without touching the router or tools. Writing the RAG diagram forced me to articulate the retrieve-augment-generate steps explicitly rather than leaving them implicit in service code. I underestimated how much the keyword fallback in `Handbook.search` influences which rule is returned — a smarter retriever (e.g., embedding similarity) would be the highest-value next upgrade. Overall, the modular design paid off: each lab deliverable touched at most two modules without cascading changes.
